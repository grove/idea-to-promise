# Recipe: did the delivery actually help?

## Situation

P2P has proved that the promised behavior was delivered, and now you want to know whether the original real-world outcome actually improved.

## Start with

```text
/discover outcome specs/<slug>.md; evidence <delivery + real-world observations>
```

## What to expect

ITP should keep two questions separate:

> Was the promised behavior delivered?

and:

> Did that behavior improve the intended outcome?

P2P evidence can answer the first question for the exact contract and candidate. It cannot, by itself, establish adoption, usefulness, reduced rework, customer value, or other real-world impact.

Outcome review should therefore record the relevant observation period, population or system context, actual observations, counterevidence, and important limitations.

## Healthy result

The outcome may be recorded as improved, mixed, no-improvement, or not-assessed.

If no real-world outcome observations exist, **not-assessed** is the correct answer even when delivery proof is completely green.

The learning can trigger a revisit, amendment, new opportunity, or no action. It never silently becomes new scope.
