# Recipe: zoom out to the bigger picture

## Situation

You have been working on a project for a while. The team is making progress, but execution has become the default and you want to ask a more important question:

> Are we still working on the right things?

This is different from revisiting one promise. You want to look across the project as a whole.

## Start with

```text
/discover zoom-out
```

Or simply say:

> We’ve been working on this for months. Help me step back and look at the bigger picture.

## What to expect

ITP should reconnect the current work to the outcomes the project was originally trying to achieve. It should look at what has changed, what has actually been learned or delivered, where attention is going now, and whether important problems or opportunities have been crowded out.

It should be willing to say that something deserves less attention even if a lot of effort has already gone into it. Past investment is evidence about cost already paid; it is not proof that continuing is the best future choice.

The result should not be a project-status dump. ITP should turn the review into a few understandable strategic directions, for example:

1. **Keep the current direction** — continue because the work still lines up well with the intended outcome.
2. **Change focus** — keep the project, but move attention to a more important part of the problem.
3. **Validate before expanding** — pause new capability and check whether the current work is producing the outcome you care about.
4. **Stop or defer something** — deliberately free capacity from work that no longer earns its place.
5. **Open a new discovery or revisit** — when the bigger-picture review reveals a genuinely new opportunity or shows that an existing promise should be reconsidered.

ITP should recommend the strongest direction or a small shortlist and explain why.

## Healthy result

You understand what still matters most, what changed, what may deserve less attention, what you may be missing, and what ITP recommends doing next.

The review itself does not silently change active decisions or approved promises. If you choose a material change, ITP carries that choice into the appropriate normal decision, revisit, or amendment flow.
