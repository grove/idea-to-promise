# Recipe: P2P found a problem with the promise

## Situation

Promise to Proof is implementing or proving an approved promise and discovers something important. The key question is whether the problem belongs to delivery or whether it means the promise itself needs to change.

## Start with

```text
/discover amend specs/<slug>.md; trigger <P2P evidence>
```

## What to expect

ITP should classify the feedback before changing scope.

If the approved promise is still right and the problem is implementation, candidate identity, CI, evidence quality, test design, or publication, the issue should normally stay in P2P.

If the promised behavior, boundary, binding constraint, or decision basis itself is wrong or materially ambiguous, the work returns to ITP.

The active approved source should remain untouched while an amendment is merely proposed.

## Healthy result

A proposed amendment clearly states what triggered it, what would change, and what remains unchanged.

A material replacement needs a new human decision when applicable, a new promise revision, exact approval, and then a fresh P2P acceptance-planning handoff.

Delivery difficulty is never automatic permission to weaken the promise.
