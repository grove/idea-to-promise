# Revisit without rewriting the old promise

> Fictional teaching example. The dialogue and source notes are authored fixtures, not live-agent output, customer research, or real approval.

## Prior state

In this fictional history D1 selected offline draft storage for a single device.
A v1 source and its example approval describe that boundary. No real approval is
asserted by this teaching document.

**User:** “Revisit offline drafts. People now switch devices. A delivery note also
says device-local recovery worked.”

## Changed evidence

The new user statement is an attributed report, not measured adoption evidence.
The delivery note can support device-local technical behavior only for its named
candidate/context; it does not establish a need for cloud sync or customer value.
O2, cross-device continuity, is distinct from O1, local recovery.

A1 preserves the existing scope; A2 investigates cross-device storage; A3 provides
a manual export/import path. Privacy and conflict behavior are load-bearing
unknowns for A2. They do not automatically become v1 requirements.

## Pending decision

**Agent:** “The prior promise remains local-only. We can investigate cross-device
use separately without changing that agreement. The key choice is whether to
spend a bounded discovery effort on O2.”

**User:** “Defer cross-device work until we have direct evidence from the affected
users. Keep the approved local recovery work unchanged.”

Record the new defer decision and trigger, retaining prior D1/source/receipt
unchanged. Do not mark v1 stale merely because an unapproved new idea exists.
A same-context check confirms no downstream scope amendment is authorized.

## A real portable handoff

For v1 delivery the receiving P2P context needs the exact source, retained approval
receipt and attributable approval text—not a broken path into another machine's
ignored notebook. Transfer those records explicitly under authorized access,
recompute the real digest and verify source path, revision and identity.

Only then propose `/plan-acceptance <v1-source>`. This teaching walkthrough does
not invoke P2P or claim its acceptance contract is already approved. A future
materially changed promise would be v2 with its own exact approval.
