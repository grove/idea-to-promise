# Shape the promise

Turn a selected direction into source material suitable for human agreement. This is not a Promise to Proof acceptance contract or implementation plan.

Read the framing, alternatives, evidence, constraints, and actual human decisions. Do not assume the leading recommendation was selected.

Choose one outcome:
- pursue,
- experiment,
- defer,
- reject.

For defer/reject, record rationale and a revisit trigger and report **NO PROMISE**.

For pursue/experiment, draft a bounded source promise containing:
- title and revision,
- intended observable outcome,
- promised behavior,
- boundaries and non-goals,
- binding constraints,
- evidence and rationale clearly marked advisory,
- alternatives and decision clearly marked advisory,
- assumptions/open questions,
- approval and handoff status.

Do not add acceptance matrices, seam/oracle selections, detailed test plans, implementation decomposition, candidate IDs, or proof verdicts. Those belong to Promise to Proof.

A consequential technical choice becomes binding only when actually agreed. An experiment promise commits to the learning activity, exposure, observation, and decision/stop rule—not to a favorable result.

Report **READY FOR APPROVAL** only when material behavior choices are resolved. Report **AGREED** only after attributable human approval of the exact source text. Changes require a new revision and renewed agreement.

After agreement, the next action is:

```text
/plan-acceptance <agreed-source-path>
```

Source approval does not authorize implementation, commit, push, or publication.
