# Opportunities and alternatives: <name>

## Opportunity space
O1 — Actor, situation, unmet need, evidence/assumption, relevance to outcome.

## Solution space
A1 — Existing/native capability or current behavior; addresses O1.
A2 — A different mechanism; addresses O1.
For each: mechanism, tradeoff, prerequisites, reversibility, critical assumption,
and cheapest useful check. These are alternatives, not human selections.

## User-facing alternatives
Present a short numbered or bulleted list. For each alternative include:
- What it means, in plain language.
- Why someone might choose it.
- The main downside.

Keep internal IDs available for traceability, but do not make the user decode them
to understand the choices.

## Recommendation
Recommend one alternative when one stands out. Otherwise recommend a small
shortlist, or the best bounded experiment/defer path when more evidence is needed.
Explain the reason and what uncertainty could change the recommendation.

This recommendation is advisory; the human still decides.
