# Frame the problem

Clarify the real need before selecting a solution or approving a promise.

## Work record

When operating in a writable project, derive a short stable slug and save the
record to .itp/work/<slug>/frame.md. Preserve an existing work item and human
edits. Local saving grants no commit, push, tracker, or publication authority.

For an informal conversation without a writable project, return the same record
inline rather than inventing persistence.

## Establish the frame

Read the idea and supplied context. Separate the need from the first proposed
solution. Establish:

- Problem or opportunity: what is happening now and why it matters.
- Actors: who experiences the problem or whose behavior must change.
- Progress sought: what the actor is trying to accomplish.
- Current workaround: what happens today instead, including doing nothing.
- Intended outcome: the observable change that would make things better.
- Behavior/change needed: what needs to become easier, possible, safer, faster,
  clearer, or different; do not name a feature unless it is already binding.
- Context and constraints: technical, legal, operational, commercial, or policy
  limits supported by current evidence.
- Decision criteria: how later alternatives will be judged.
- Status-quo consequence: what happens if nothing changes.
- Assumptions and material unknowns.
- Research bounds: time, cost, access, privacy, or scope limits.

Attribute supplied reports rather than turning them into observations. Mark
inferred context as an assumption.

Ask only questions whose answers could materially change the next step. If missing
information is not load-bearing, continue with explicit assumptions.

## Output

Use headings: Problem or opportunity; Actors; Progress sought; Current workaround;
Intended outcome; Behavior/change needed; Context and constraints; Decision
criteria; Status-quo consequence; Assumptions; Material unknowns; Research bounds;
Next action.

Save and reread the record before reporting a durable handoff.

Do not choose a solution, implement product code, publish externally, create a P2P
acceptance contract, or claim that a framed outcome is already an agreed promise.

End with Next steps: and numbered applicable options, normally /brainstorm
.itp/work/<slug>/frame.md and focused research when an unknown is already clearly
decision-critical.
