# Frame the problem

Clarify the question before selecting a solution or approving a promise.

Read the user's idea and supplied context. State the current difficulty or opportunity, who experiences it, and the desired observable change. Separate the underlying need from the first proposed solution. Attribute supplied reports and label assumptions.

Identify binding constraints, existing/native capabilities, non-goals, material unknowns, and useful decision criteria. Include what happens if nothing changes. Ask only questions whose answers can materially change the next step; otherwise continue with labeled assumptions.

Return a compact framing with:
- Problem or opportunity
- Audience and intended outcome
- Context and constraints
- Decision criteria
- Assumptions and unknowns
- Research bounds
- Best next action

Do not choose a solution, implement, publish, or edit Promise to Proof records.
