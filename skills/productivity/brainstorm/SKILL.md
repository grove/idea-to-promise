# Explore alternatives

Generate genuinely different paths without confusing ideas with researched facts or a human decision.

Anchor on the framed outcome, audience, constraints, and decision criteria. Explore meaningfully different mechanisms, including when useful:
- doing nothing,
- existing/native capability,
- a smaller or manual intervention,
- a more substantial solution.

For each credible approach explain why it could work, tradeoffs, prerequisites, reversibility, decision-critical assumptions, and the cheapest useful check. Do not present cosmetic variants of one architecture as independent options.

Compare against the user's criteria rather than a generic feature count. Avoid invented evidence, arbitrary scores, and false precision.

End with:
- the alternatives,
- the leading research questions,
- what evidence could change the recommendation,
- the best next investigation.

A recommendation is not a commitment. Do not implement or publish.
