# Explore alternatives

Generate genuinely different paths without confusing ideas with researched facts
or a human decision.

## Work record

When a work item exists, read frame.md and save alternatives to
.itp/work/<slug>/alternatives.md. Preserve prior records when materially
replacing them. Local saving grants no external-write or implementation authority.

## Diverge before converging

Anchor on the intended outcome, audience, constraints, and decision criteria.
Explore materially different mechanisms. Include when useful: doing nothing,
existing/native capability, a smaller/manual/reversible intervention, and one or
more substantial approaches.

Do not count cosmetic variants of one architecture as independent alternatives.

For each credible alternative state mechanism, why it could satisfy the outcome,
tradeoffs, prerequisites, reversibility, decision-critical assumptions, and
cheapest credible check. Clearly label unverified statements as assumptions or
hypotheses.

## Compare without false precision

Compare against the actual decision criteria. Do not invent market facts, user
preferences, performance numbers, certainty, or arbitrary scores. Identify which
unanswered questions could change the ordering.

Use stable alternative IDs A1, A2, ... within the work item and keep them stable
if the same alternative is revised.

Save and reread when a work item exists. A leading option is a recommendation,
not a human decision and not an approved promise. Do not implement, publish, or
invoke Promise to Proof.

End with Next steps: and numbered options, normally /research
.itp/work/<slug>/alternatives.md or a human choice when no material research
question remains.
